Portfolio history

No real or historical series is connected in this public demo.

For a live build, store dated snapshots and track external contributions and withdrawals separately. Do not infer investment performance from changes in balances without accounting for cash flows.

How a real version could work

  1. Connect financial data sources you authorize, such as Monarch through its available MCP integration.
  2. Retrieve account balances and holdings using read-only operations.
  3. Supplement unsupported or incomplete fields from other authorized sources or manually provided statements.
  4. Normalize account names, ownership, currencies, identifiers and source timestamps.
  5. Reconcile totals, classify holdings, and flag stale or missing information.
  6. Generate a dashboard and validate totals, freshness and assumptions before use.

Limitations

Connector access and data coverage vary. A balance feed may not include full holdings or history. Quotes, private investments, physical assets and manually maintained balances may need separate sources and explicit valuation dates. This demo is static, fictional, makes no network requests, and is not financial advice.
